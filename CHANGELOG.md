# Changelog

All notable changes to this fork are listed here. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org):
`vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`, one git tag per version. haprod is a fork of
[Daryl](https://rickub.com/hlassiege/daryl) by Hugo Lassiège; "upstream" below means Daryl.

## v0.3.1 (2026-11-16)

- Edit step: a field in the toolbar finds a spoken phrase on the timeline and moves the playhead there
  (Enter again goes to the next place).
- Suggested sounds: the brief given to Claude is kept from one project to the next.
- Fix: the picture search could not index iPhone footage (HEVC with limited-range colors), and one
  unreadable clip stopped the indexing of all the following ones.

## v0.3.0 (2026-11-16)

### Sound

- The library searches royalty-free sounds and music online (Freesound and Jamendo, through Openverse,
  with no key), limited to licenses usable on a monetized channel: public domain and CC BY.
- Each downloaded sound keeps its author and license. The Details step lists the credits CC BY requires,
  inserts them in the description, and a check stays red until they are there.
- Media page of a project: Claude suggests music and sound effects from the script and the footage, each
  with royalty-free results to listen to and add.
- Claude can also choose one sound per idea and place it on the edit timeline, anchored on what is said.
  It chooses from titles and tags, without hearing: the placement is a proposal to listen to, and it can
  be undone.

### Search in the footage

- Rough cut step, Search tab: find a passage by describing what is seen in it, with a multilingual vision
  model (CLIP) that runs on the machine, or what is said in it, from the transcripts.
- New requirements: `open_clip_torch`, `transformers`, `sentencepiece`; the model (about 1.5 GB) is
  downloaded on first use.

### Known limits

- Placed sounds use fixed volumes; music is not ducked under the voice.
- The picture search always returns its twelve closest pictures, relevant or not.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-16)

First version of the fork, based on upstream commit `89fef88` (package version 0.1.0).

### macOS

- Runs on macOS: native file and folder dialogs (AppleScript), reveal in Finder, Chrome lookup,
  Homebrew paths for ffmpeg, npx and Claude Code, system fonts for on-screen texts.
- The fal.ai key is read from the macOS Keychain (Windows keeps the user environment).
- `ffmpeg-full` is preferred when installed, with a clear message when the `drawtext` filter is missing.

### Performance on Apple Silicon

- Whisper runs on the GPU through mlx-whisper, cut at silences in chunks of 30 s at most, with a filter
  for text invented on background noise.
- Video is encoded by VideoToolbox (`STUDIO_ENCODER=x264` goes back to libx264).

### Claude and ChatGPT

- Connect and disconnect the Claude account from Settings; same for ChatGPT (Codex).
- Choose the Claude model, separately for light steps and heavy steps.
- Every Claude turn of a project step is logged with its usage in `data/claude-usage.jsonl`.
- Thumbnails without fal.ai: generated by ChatGPT (subscription), or composed by Claude from your images.

### Fixes

- `httpx` was missing from the requirements (the worker crashed on start).
- PyAV is kept below 19, which breaks faster-whisper 1.2.
