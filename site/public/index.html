Fits the way you already work

In your language
Daryl writes your titles, scripts and descriptions in the language of your channel.
Your footage stays on your disk
Transcription and rendering run on your machine. Your rushes go nowhere until you publish.
Keep your editing software
Send the rough cut or the edit to DaVinci Resolve or Premiere and finish it there.

One command for the most tedious jobs

Open your AI agent in the repository and type the command.

  • /music-bedMusic under the whole video, cut to length and ducked under your voice.
  • /replace-aroll-takeRe-filmed a passage? Drop it in, and the B-roll, zooms and sounds follow.
  • /snatch-cutA burst of close-ups, snap zooms and punchy sounds to wake up a sequence.

A personal tool, shared as is

It follows how I work and evolves in that direction. It is maintained on a best-effort basis. Fork it, take what you need, or use it for ideas.

Try it

You need Windows, Python 3.12, Node.js with pnpm, ffmpeg and Claude Code logged in.

git clone https://rickub.com/hlassiege/daryl.git
cd daryl
py -3.12 -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\pip install -r backend\requirements.txt
cd web; pnpm install; pnpm build; cd ..\backend
..\.venv\Scripts\python -m studio

Then open http://localhost:4400. Read the README for the optional services: cloud transcription, thumbnails, YouTube upload, music.
